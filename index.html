About the label

Rooted in the surf and skate life

Hapsay Lifestyle is a Philippine clothing label built around one idea: what you wear should say something. Its oversized tees pair clean fronts with loud, thoughtful back prints, from the star-and-eye Vision graphic to reminders to get off your phone and chase your dreams.

The label sells the grassroots way: drops announced on Facebook, pop-up booths like Frights & Finds, and orders packed and shipped by the team to a growing community of wearers in Cebu, Davao, and beyond.

How to order

Hapsay takes orders on Messenger, the same way its community already shops. Three steps and your tee is on its way.

01

Pick your tee

Choose a design and colorway from The Drop above, and your size from Small to XL. Oversized fit, so size down if you want it closer.

02

Message the Page

Tap any Order button to open Messenger. Send the tee name, color, and size. The team confirms the price and availability with you there.

03

Pay, then it ships

Orders are payment first, then packed and shipped to your door. Free Hapsay stickers ride along with every order.

Good to know

Questions

How much are the tees?

Prices are confirmed on Messenger when you order, since drops and pop-up stocks change. Send the tee name and size and the team will quote you right away.

What sizes are available?

Tees come in Small to XL with an oversized, unisex fit. If you are between sizes or want a closer fit, ask on Messenger and the team will help you pick.

How do I pay?

Orders are payment first, confirmed on Messenger, before your order is packed and shipped. The team will walk you through the payment options in chat.

Do you ship outside your city?

Yes. Orders are packed and shipped to customers around the Philippines, including the brand's community in Cebu and Davao.

What comes with my order?

Your tee, plus free Hapsay stickers with every order. Pop-up collections are limited runs, so once a design sells out it may not come back.
