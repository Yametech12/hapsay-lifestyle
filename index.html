About the label

Rooted in the surf and skate life

Hapsay Lifestyle is a Philippine clothing label built around one idea: what you wear should say something. Its oversized tees pair clean fronts with loud, thoughtful back prints, from the star-and-eye Vision graphic to a reminder to get out of your phone.

The label sells the grassroots way: drops announced on Facebook, pop-up booths like Frights & Finds, and orders packed and shipped by the team to wearers in Cebu and Davao.

Before you order

How much are the tees?

Prices are confirmed on Messenger when you order, since drops and pop-up stocks change. Send the tee name and size and the team will quote you right away.

What sizes are available?

Tees come in Small to XL with an oversized fit. If you are between sizes, ask on Messenger and the team will help you pick.

How do I pay?

Orders are payment first, confirmed on Messenger, before your order is packed and shipped. The team will walk you through the payment options in chat.

Do you ship outside your city?

Yes. Orders are packed and shipped after payment, and Hapsay wearers in Cebu and Davao have received theirs. Message the Page and the team will confirm shipping to your address.

What comes with my order?

Your tee, plus free Hapsay stickers with every order.
