About the label

Rooted in the surf and skate life

Hapsay Lifestyle is a Philippine clothing label built around one idea: what you wear should say something. Its oversized tees pair clean fronts with loud, thoughtful back prints, from the star-and-eye Vision graphic to a reminder to get out of your phone.

The label sells the grassroots way: drops announced on Facebook, pop-up booths like Frights & Finds, and orders packed and shipped by the team to wearers in Cebu and Davao.

How to order

Hapsay takes orders on Messenger, the same way its community already shops. Three steps and your tee is on its way. Prices are confirmed on Messenger, just ask.

01

Pick your tee

Choose a design and colorway from The Drop above, and your size from Small to XL.

02

Message the Page

Tap any Order button to open Messenger. Send the tee name, color, and size. The team confirms the price and availability with you there.

03

Pay, then it ships

Orders are payment first, then packed and shipped. Free Hapsay stickers ride along with every order.
